# Recorded machine-gun source

`ak47-recorded-burst.wav` is `Prepared SFX Library/AK-47/C_27P.wav`
from **The Free Firearm Sound Library**.

Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney.
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public-domain dedication).

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Mirror: https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Archive: https://lpc.opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Prepared%20SFX%20Library.7z
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source metadata: AK-47, rifle, fully automatic, semi automatic, machine gun,
assault rifle, 7.62x39, gunshots, long burst, near distance, front of shooter,
left, right, stereo.

`tools/build_machine_gun_sound.py` builds a sustained automatic-fire burst,
folds it to mono, filters rumble/harshness, and gently compresses the body
before reducing peak level to -34 dBFS. Runtime streams this recording in
20 ms chunks, retaining its natural firing rhythm. The older individual
reports are retained for comparison but are no longer used by the game.
Original recording is preserved here for local rebuilding with ffmpeg.

Gunfire tuning: the recorded burst plays at 88% pitch (about two semitones lower), with stronger 70–240 Hz impacts, reduced upper crack and lighter compression to preserve punch. Peak output is -34 dBFS.

Sustained fire loops an uninterrupted run of recorded rounds, trimmed before the next shot with matched tail endpoints. No burst-end silence or whole-loop fade; it continues until firing stops.

## Reaper encounter effects

`../reaper/*.wav` combines original filtered noise/resonances with the project's
original `omacontra-start-impact.wav` explosion and the CC0-derived
`machine-gun-1.wav` report credited above. No additional third-party recordings
are used. `tools/build_reaper_sounds.py` reproduces all 19 assets deterministically.
These are subdued arcade effects; no voice recordings are included.

## Stage-two effects

`../chase/*.wav` is generated by `tools/build_chase_sounds.py` from original
filtered noise, short engine/actuator resonances and the project's revised
`reaper/break.wav` and `reaper/rupture.wav` pressure-blast textures. These revised
explosion textures are original synthesis; no additional recordings are used.

## Replacement weapon and destruction palette (current)

The old shared generated thuds are superseded by `tools/sound_palette.py`.
Source masters are kept locally for reproducible rebuilds:

- `kenney-scifi/*.ogg`: **Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0**, Kenney, CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  Original license: `kenney-scifi/License.txt`.
  Uses explosionCrunch, lowFrequency_explosion, laserSmall, laserLarge,
  thrusterFire and forceField families. No impactMetal samples are used.
- `model12-recorded.wav`: `Prepared SFX Library/Model 12/K_17P.wav`, from
  The Free Firearm Sound Library credited at the top of this file (CC0).
  Actual reports begin near 0.903 and 7.054 seconds; trimmed locally for the
  cannon/mortar. This does not replace the player's automatic-fire recording.

Processing: event-specific source layers, attack/tail trims, mild treble
softening, quiet peak normalization. Alternate source takes cycle in the mixer.
Reaper eye and raven destruction now use separate events. Large explosions
retain long tails; weapon launches have their own distinct source families.

## Tidebreaker water and deck palette

- `water/ocean-splash.ogg`: **Ocean Splash**, Thimras, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/ocean-splash
- `water/ezwa-water_splash/water_splash-01.flac` through `06.flac`: **6 Short Water Splashes**, ezwa (submitted by qubodup), CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/6-short-water-splashes
- Guardian weapons use the recorded Model 12 and Kenney sources credited above.
- `tools/build_tide_sounds.py` trims, layers, filters and attenuates those recordings, creates three variations for water/guardian events, and synthesizes quiet wooden caster/deck foley. DHH movement reuses the established first-level palette. No hollow metal impact is used as the common kill/weapon sound.
