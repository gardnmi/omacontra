# Guardian meteor

`guardian-meteor.png` — generated using the built-in imagegen tool, with native alpha. Runtime artwork caches the sprite at 80×190, animates the flame wake separately from the rock core, and adds deck impact steam and fragments. These effects do not enlarge the damage area.

Prompt: Production game sprite on genuinely transparent alpha background. Single spectacular FALLING METEOR pointing vertically DOWN, not a missile or arrow. Chunky irregular dark obsidian asteroid core in lower quarter, cracked with intense white hot cyan plasma, glowing blue corona around jagged stone. Broad turbulent comet tail rises straight UP, curling cyan, cobalt and white plasma flames and small glowing embers, tapering at top. Rich shaded hand-crafted pixel art matching high quality 16-bit arcade boss sprites, hard pixel clusters, strong volume and contrast. Core large and round, tail approximately three times core height; entire silhouette width about 1/3 height. One centered isolated sprite, whole object visible, tight transparent margins. No backdrop, no ground, no cast shadow, no text, no logos, no panels. Asset should feel heavy and dangerous, not thin lightning.
